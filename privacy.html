Privacy Policy

K4 Control (Alexa skill) · Effective 6 September 2026

This skill collects no personal information about you. There is no data to disclose, sell, or lose.

What the skill does

K4 Control lets you switch an Elecraft K4 transceiver on or off, and ask whether it is on, by voice. When you speak a command, Alexa determines which of three intents you meant and passes that intent to a small AWS Lambda function. The function translates it into a single command name — power_on, power_off, or power_query — and relays that name to a bridge program running on the radio's own local network. The bridge is the only component that talks to the radio. Nothing else is transmitted.

Information collected

None. Specifically, the skill:

  • does not ask for, collect, or store your name, email address, postal address, telephone number, or any other personal information;
  • uses no account linking and maintains no user accounts of its own;
  • does not store voice recordings or transcripts of anything you say;
  • does not read your Alexa profile, contacts, calendar, location, or device settings;
  • uses no cookies, advertising identifiers, tracking pixels, or analytics services;
  • does not share, sell, rent, or transfer information to anyone — there is none to share.

As it does for every skill, Alexa includes an anonymous, skill-specific user identifier in each request. K4 Control does not read, use, or retain that identifier.

Diagnostic logs

The Lambda function writes brief operational log entries — the type of request received, which of the three commands it mapped to, and an automatically generated request identifier — so that faults can be diagnosed. These entries contain no personal information, are visible only to the operator of this skill, and are used for no other purpose.

Amazon's role

Your interaction with Alexa itself — the wake word, voice recordings, and your voice history — is handled by Amazon under Amazon's own privacy notice, not by this skill. You can review and delete that history in the Alexa app or at amazon.com/alexaprivacy.

Children

This skill is not directed to children. It collects no information from anyone, children included.
